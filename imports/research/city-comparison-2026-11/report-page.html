Per resident, Greater Manchester fell from — to — tonnes and Seattle from — (2008) to — (2022). Chicago's tonnages are approximate: they apply the city's published percentage cuts to its 2005 baseline of 36.2 Mt.

  • Greater Manchester's decline came mostly from homes, commerce and industry as the UK grid dropped coal; transport emissions were flat until 2020 and have stayed about 15% lower since.
  • DC credits greener regional electricity and more efficient commercial buildings for most of its cut. New York's buildings still produce about two-thirds of its emissions.
  • Seattle's electricity is already mostly hydropower, so its emissions are dominated by road transport (about 58%), which fell only during the pandemic.
  • These are territorial (production-based) inventories. Consumption-based accounting is much larger: Seattle estimates about 16 t CO₂e per resident once goods and services consumed are counted.
Definitions, methods and source documents
  • Greater Manchester school meals: DfE "Schools, pupils and their characteristics" (January census, state-funded schools), summed over Bolton, Bury, Manchester, Oldham, Rochdale, Salford, Stockport, Tameside, Trafford and Wigan.

New York: NYC DOE Demographic Snapshot, district schools (excludes charters). Chicago: CPS 20th-day "Free/Reduced Lunch" / "Economically Disadvantaged" report.   • Prices: BLS CPI-U (via FRED series CUURA423SAM, CUURA207SAM, CUURA101SAM, CPIMEDNS and matching all-items series); ONS series D7BZ and D7BT. Premiums: AHRQ MEPS-IC Tables II.D.1 and II.D.2 (2015–2020 from AHRQ; 2021 and 2023–2025 as tabulated by KFF State Health Facts from MEPS-IC). UK spending: ONS UK Health Accounts reference tables, Table 1c.
  • Temperature: NOAA NCEI Climate at a Glance city series (USW00024233, USW00094846, USW00094728, USW00013743); Met Office HadUK-Grid "England NW and N Wales". CO₂: NOAA GML Mauna Loa annual means. Air quality: EPA AirData annual AQI by CBSA; DEFRA AURN Manchester Piccadilly.
  • Emissions: DESNZ UK local authority GHG statistics 2005–2024; NYC Greenhouse Gas Emissions Inventory (NYC Open Data); DC DOEE 2006–2023 inventory workbook; Seattle OSE 2022 Community GHG Inventory (core sectors summed from Appendix A, before carbon offsets); City of Chicago 2017 GHG Inventory Report (−11.0% in 2015 and −16.9% in 2017 vs 2005) and the 2008 Chicago Climate Action Plan baseline.
