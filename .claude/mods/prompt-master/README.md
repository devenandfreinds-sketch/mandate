# Prompt Master

Rewrites your rough prompt into a clear, well-structured one before you send it.

- **Button**: type a rough prompt, then press **✨ Prompt Master** in the band just above the prompt box (or focus the band and press `p`). The rewrite replaces your draft. Review it, then press Enter. **Undo** (`u`) brings back your original.
- **Command**: `/prompt-master <rough prompt>` works on every surface, mobile included. The rewrite lands in the prompt box.

The rewrite keeps your intent and every concrete detail (paths, names, errors) and never invents requirements. It runs one Sonnet call through your session.
